# Pebblecount

## Streaks that stick.

Pebblecount is the tiny habit tracker for people who are tired of big habit trackers. Drop a pebble in the jar every day you show up. Watch the jar fill. That's it. That's the app.

### Why people love the jar

- **One pebble a day.** Check in once per habit per day. No points, no levels, no confetti cannons.
- **Streaks with a heart.** Miss a day? Your Skip Stone has your back. Every habit gets one forgiven miss in any rolling 7 days, so a single bad Tuesday never wipes out a great month.
- **Your pebbles are yours.** Export everything to a single JSON file whenever you like. Leave any time and take your jar with you.
- **Lives in your terminal.** Pebblecount ships a small command-line tool, `pebble`, for people who would rather type than tap.
